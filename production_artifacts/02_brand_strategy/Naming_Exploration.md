# Naming Exploration — Collision-Screened Rework

## Status and boundary

The original shortlist is rejected after external collision screening. This rework develops a fresh retailer-name pool for a curated baking ingredients and supplies store—not a bakery, café, marketplace or appliance maker.

Screening is preliminary only. It is not trademark, MCA company-name, linguistic, domain or social-handle clearance. No name is confirmed. Formal professional clearance remains mandatory before real commercial use.

## Screening method

Live web screening was performed on **2026-08-24 (Asia/Kolkata)**. For every candidate, the following seven checks were run and inspected for obvious business/social use:

1. exact quoted name;
2. exact name + `bakery`;
3. exact name + `baking`;
4. exact name + `food`;
5. exact name + `kitchen`;
6. exact name + `India`;
7. exact `.com` string/direct status where observable.

Search results can be incomplete, personalized or stale. “No relevant indexed result observed” does not mean availability. Direct `.com` states are recorded only where surfaced; no unobserved domain is called available.

## Screened shortlist

### 1. Pantryform — recommended

A compact retailer-level compound: “pantry” signals ingredients and readiness; “form” opens into tools, moulds, packaging, method and visual structure. It fits the approved measured-abundance world without describing a finished bakery. Preliminary search found no exact baking/food/kitchen/India business, but `.com` ownership was not verified and the adjacent packaging name Packform requires professional similarity review.

**Prototype direction only—formal legal clearance pending.**

### 2. The Making Shelf

Broad enough for ingredients, equipment, packaging and recipes; makes curation tangible and supports retailer-led architecture. Exact screening found no relevant business collision. Remaining risks: three words, article handling in URLs/lockups, and a possible craft-retail interpretation.

### 3. Method Pantry

Balances useful guidance with assortment breadth and works naturally with Measured Joy. Exact screening found descriptive content rather than same-name businesses. Remaining risks: slightly editorial, and “method” may feel procedural without sensory execution.

### 4. Supply & Stir

Immediately signals retail supply with an active making cue; easy to hear and spell. No relevant exact-name collision was observed. Remaining risks: “supply” can feel utilitarian/B2B and “stir” only represents one baking action.

### 5. ReadyMeasure

Connects preparedness and precision, reinforcing the approved position. No same-name baking/food/kitchen business was observed, but the exact `.com` is registered and listed for sale (S-14), so this is a caution candidate. It may also sound like a measurement service.

## Recommendation

Advance **Pantryform** for prototype identity exploration only, paired with the descriptor “Baking ingredients & supplies” until recognition is tested. Do not treat it as selected or cleared.

**Prototype direction only—formal legal clearance pending.**

## Explicit exclusions

Whisklane, Crumbloom, Batterfolk and Ovenora are rejected and not advanced; no close spelling variants appear in this pool. Pantryrise was not preserved automatically and did not enter the fresh shortlist. No retailer name is confirmed.
