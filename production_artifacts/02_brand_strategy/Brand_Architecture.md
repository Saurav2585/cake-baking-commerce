# Brand Architecture

## Model

Use a **retailer-led, multi-brand architecture**. The proposed retailer name is the primary trust and navigation layer. Original fictional product brands identify ranges and manufacturers within the catalog; they do not become parallel storefronts or sellers.

## Hierarchy

1. **Retailer brand:** master identity, experience promise, service voice and commerce disclosure.
2. **Departments:** Ingredients; Chocolate; Colours & Flavours; Fillings & Fondant; Decorating; Bakeware & Tools; Packaging; Recipes (D-015).
3. **Product brands:** secondary discovery/filter metadata and optional range landing routes.
4. **Product family and product:** factual category/name layer.
5. **Variant/SKU:** pack, colour, size, dimension or count with variant-level price and availability.

## Naming syntax

- Navigation and service language lead with the retailer.
- Product presentation uses: `[product brand] + [factual product name] + [variant]`.
- Product brands must not be presented as sellers, endorsed partners or retailer-owned unless the fictional-data model explicitly records that relationship.
- Recipes live under the retailer editorial layer and may map to products without implying endorsement.

## Product-brand role

Later catalog work may create multiple original fictional brands to make comparison realistic. Each needs a narrow rationale and distinct name, but no invented founding story, certification, expertise, sourcing pedigree, popularity, review history or retailer endorsement.

## Endorsement and relationship rules

- Allowed: “Available from [retailer]” or neutral brand metadata in a demo context.
- Avoid: “approved by”, “official”, “exclusive”, “trusted partner”, “our experts recommend” unless a documented relationship/evidence exists.
- Retailer-owned private label is deferred unless separately approved and clearly modelled.
- The interface must not imply marketplace seller choice (D-012).

## Extension rules

The architecture can later accommodate content collections, seasonal edits, business-size packs and a private label without renaming departments. Any extension must retain retailer primacy, factual product data and clear relationship labels.

## Governance

Final retailer and product-brand names require separate legal, linguistic, domain and handle validation. Brand architecture approval does not establish availability or ownership.
