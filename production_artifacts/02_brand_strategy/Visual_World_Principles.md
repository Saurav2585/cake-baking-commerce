# Visual World Principles

This document defines strategic territory, not a UI, moodboard, logo or production art direction.

## Core visual idea: measured abundance

Show the energy and variety of baking through deliberate order: ingredients in useful groupings, tools in purposeful relation, and moments of transformation. The world should feel generous but never cluttered; exact but never clinical.

## Principles

1. **Material before motif.** Lead with texture, form, scale and process—not cupcakes, chef hats or novelty icons.
2. **Order creates confidence.** Grids, sequences, labelled groupings and measured spacing may express expertise without authority claims.
3. **Warmth comes from making.** Hands, folds, pours, finishes and packed outcomes may add humanity; avoid staged “perfect family” testimony.
4. **Colour earns a role.** Use appetising, ingredient-led depth with controlled bright accents. Avoid default pink/pastel coding and rainbow overload.
5. **Premium is clarity.** Cropping, light, typography and material detail create value; dark luxury codes, gold excess and obscure editorialism do not.
6. **Products remain legible.** Packaging shape, pack scale and relevant distinctions must not disappear inside decorative compositions.

## Image-content boundaries

- Prefer original/generated process still life, ingredient textures, measured arrangements and bake-in-progress moments.
- Keep edible and non-edible decoration visibly distinct.
- Do not depict certifications, branded competitor packs, unsupported outcomes, unsafe handling or impossible product performance.
- Generated assets require Asset Manifest provenance before release (D-006).

## System implications for later design

- The retailer brand should hold the page structure; fictional product brands function as secondary identifiers.
- Photography/illustration should support the eight-department system without assigning a rigid stereotype to each category.
- Accessibility governs contrast, text over imagery, motion alternatives and information conveyed by colour.
- Visual exploration must test desktop, tablet and mobile without converting this strategy into a fixed layout.

## Originality check

Later design must compare concepts against R-001 and the Phase 1 “must not transfer” list. No reference-site composition, iconography, promotional rail system or styling is prescribed here.
