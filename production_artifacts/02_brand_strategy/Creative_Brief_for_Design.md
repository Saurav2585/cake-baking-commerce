# Creative Brief for Design

## Assignment

After approval, translate the selected retailer name and brand territory into an original identity and UI-direction exploration for an India-focused curated baking-supplies store. This brief does not authorize that work during Phase 2A.

## Audience

Primary Indian home bakers/hobbyists; secondary micro-bakeries and small operators (D-010). Preferences remain hypotheses, so concepts should be evaluated rather than presented as user-validated.

## Desired response

“This feels like a baking specialist I can understand, enjoy and use.”

## Single-minded proposition

Everything for the bake, made easier to choose.

## Recommended strategic inputs

- Name: **Pantryform**, approved for portfolio/demo prototype use only; formal legal clearance remains pending.
- Territory: **Measured Joy**, externally approved.
- Personality: capable, encouraging, considered, sensory, straightforward.
- Visual idea: measured abundance.

## Required design behaviours

- Establish retailer primacy while keeping product brands legible and subordinate.
- Express specialist depth without trade-catalogue intimidation.
- Combine exactness and sensory warmth across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Support all eight departments without relying on colour alone.
- Protect readable product/variant information and WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented contrast/focus needs.
- Define a clear system for factual, guidance, simulation and “Information not provided” content.
- Make reduced motion and non-image fallbacks part of the concept.

## Avoid

- Childish cupcake clichés, generic pink/pastel systems, competitor-derived layouts or language.
- Gold/dark luxury shorthand, ornamental obscurity, founder mythology or expert badges.
- Unsupported quality, result, food, health, dietary, popularity, sustainability or certification claims.
- Marketplace seller prominence or a manufacturer-owned appearance.

## Expected later outputs

Naming lockup exploration after clearance, typography/color/image principles, responsive key screens, component direction, accessibility annotations, asset-generation brief and originality review. These belong to later approved phases.

## Evaluation criteria

Originality; name/position fit; department extensibility; product-brand hierarchy; accessibility intent; responsive resilience; sensory/practical balance; claim integrity; feasibility for Next.js/Tailwind/GSAP.

## Mandatory preconditions

1. Human approval of a name and territory.
2. Independent name validation before production adoption.
3. Phase 2B architecture and later design authorization.
