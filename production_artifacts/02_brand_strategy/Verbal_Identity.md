# Verbal Identity

## Voice idea

**Clear enough to choose. Warm enough to begin.**

Use contemporary Indian English: familiar spellings such as “colour” and “flavour”, plain sentence construction, and specialist terms only where they improve selection.

## Voice dimensions

| Dimension | Default | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Specific and explanatory | Lecturing, vague authority, invented expertise |
| Warmth | Encouraging and human | Childishness, excessive exclamation, forced cheer |
| Appetite | Sensory but functional | Unsupported taste, result or quality claims |
| Economy | Concise and scannable | Dense catalog jargon or decorative prose |
| Integrity | Explicit about source and uncertainty | Implication through omission |

## Writing rules

- Start with the user’s task: “For colouring chocolate”, “Choose a box size”, “Scale this recipe”.
- Name the relevant difference: medium, form, pack, dimensions, count or use.
- Prefer verbs: choose, measure, fold, fill, finish, pack.
- Use numerals and standardized units in commerce contexts.
- Call the checkout a demo/simulation wherever transactional ambiguity could arise.
- Use **“Information not provided”** exactly for missing critical ingredient, allergen and storage fields.
- Never silently turn absence into “not applicable”, “free from” or “safe”.

## Message examples

These are directional examples, not production copy.

- **Department entry:** “Start with what you’re making, then narrow by form, use and pack.”
- **Variant guidance:** “You need 180 g. The selected 250 g pack leaves 70 g.”
- **Unavailable mapping:** “No available pack matches this ingredient. Review alternatives before adding.”
- **Empty search:** “No exact match. Try a product type, use or pack size.”
- **Simulation disclosure:** “Demo checkout—no payment will be taken and no order will be fulfilled.”

## Tagline routes for later testing

- “Make your next bake clearer.”
- “Choose well. Bake ready.”
- “From measure to make.”

These are exploratory lines, not approved claims or final lockups.

## Vocabulary

**Prefer:** baking supplies, ingredients, pack, use, fit, selected, required, available, compare, recipe, tools, finish.  
**Use carefully:** specialist, curated, professional, premium, trusted, quality. These may imply evidence and should generally stay internal unless substantiated.  
**Avoid:** best, guaranteed, foolproof, healthiest, safe, authentic, pure, certified, guilt-free, top-selling, loved by bakers.
