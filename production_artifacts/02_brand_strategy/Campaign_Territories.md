# Campaign Territories

These are distinct strategic platforms for later concept exploration, not campaigns, promises or approved production copy.

## Territory A — Measured Joy (recommended)

**Idea:** confidence and pleasure meet in the small acts that make a bake possible.  
**Human tension:** inspiration is exciting; choosing compatible formats and packs can interrupt it.  
**Narrative:** measure, choose, make, enjoy—the practical sequence is part of the pleasure.  
**Verbal cues:** “From measure to make”; “Ready for the next step”; exact quantities beside sensory verbs.  
**Visual cues:** ordered mise-en-place shifting into pour/fold/finish moments; measured spacing; warm ingredient colour with precise annotation.  
**Commerce expression:** required, selected and leftover quantities become useful confidence cues, not cost pressure.  
**Why it fits:** balances home-baker warmth with micro-bakery precision and directly supports D-016.  
**Risk:** could become instructional or sterile; preserve tactility and human imperfection.

## Territory B — The Working Pantry

**Idea:** a well-chosen baking pantry is a practical creative system.  
**Human tension:** broad assortments make it hard to know what belongs together.  
**Narrative:** stock with purpose, see relationships, and move easily from ingredient to tool to pack.  
**Verbal cues:** “Build the bake”; “Everything in its useful place”; task-based labels.  
**Visual cues:** modular shelves/grids, family groupings, restock rhythms, scale and count markers.  
**Commerce expression:** comparison, repeat choice and cross-department completeness.  
**Why it fits:** strongest for micro-bakery usefulness and eight-department coherence.  
**Risk:** may feel utilitarian or bulk-led for occasional home bakers.

## Territory C — Ingredient Theatre

**Idea:** the transformation of textures, colours and forms deserves the spotlight.  
**Human tension:** baking retail can flatten sensory materials into undifferentiated pack shots.  
**Narrative:** each material has a role; choosing the right one sets the scene for making.  
**Verbal cues:** “Choose your next transformation”; pour, melt, fold, fill, finish.  
**Visual cues:** close crops, suspended transitions, controlled dramatic light, material contrasts and sparse captions.  
**Commerce expression:** application and compatibility guide discovery through sensory entry points.  
**Why it fits:** creates premium-accessible appetite without generic cupcake imagery.  
**Risk:** may become obscure luxury or imply performance; factual labels and accessible clarity must anchor it.

## Recommendation and gate

Advance **Measured Joy** for later design exploration. Preserve Working Pantry as a functional counterweight and Ingredient Theatre as an image-language reference—not a blended compromise. Human approval is required before any territory directs visual identity work.
