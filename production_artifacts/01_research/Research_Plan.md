# Phase 1 Research Plan

**Package:** UX-R1  
**Research date:** 2026-08-24 (Asia/Kolkata)  
**Owner:** UX Research; PM synthesis  
**Status:** Complete

## Objective

Identify original, evidence-backed requirements for an India-focused curated multi-brand baking-supplies demo. The references inform assortment, terminology, decision-support and commerce patterns only.

## Approved framing

- Primary audience: Indian home bakers and hobbyists.
- Secondary audience: micro-bakeries, home-baking businesses and small professional operators.
- English-only v1 with localization-ready structures.
- Curated multi-brand retailer; not a marketplace.
- Original realistic fictional brands/products with clearly demo-oriented INR prices.

## Questions

1. How should customers browse, search, filter and compare a broad baking assortment?
2. Which universal and family-specific attributes support confident selection?
3. How should pack variants, unit price, stock and temperature-sensitive handling appear?
4. Which mobile and accessibility requirements should constrain later design?
5. How should a recipe translate quantities into reviewable cart selections?

## Method and sample

- Used the same evaluation frame for BakIndia and Bakerykart: navigation, search, homepage, PLP, PDP, variants, wishlist/cart signals, recipes/content, trust/delivery, mobile and accessibility signals.
- Conducted read-only live browser walkthroughs at 1280×720 and 390×844 where pages rendered.
- Inspected representative home, chocolate listing, chocolate PDP and recipe-detail surfaces.
- Supplemented live checks with search-indexed page text when a page had earlier access limitations.
- Consulted FSSAI’s official Labelling and Display materials only to support the need to retain source-verified ingredient, allergen, net-quantity and storage fields; this is not legal advice.
- Recorded observations as paraphrases. No bulk scraping, account creation, cart mutation, transaction, screenshot retention or reference-asset reuse occurred.

## Evidence classes

- **OBSERVATION:** directly visible in a referenced page during the review.
- **EXTERNAL FACT:** supported by a named authoritative source outside the two references.
- **INFERENCE:** analyst interpretation of observations.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** unvalidated statement about users or demand.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** proposed original product requirement.

## Ethics and content controls

- Do not copy branding, prose, imagery, page composition or distinctive UI.
- Do not transfer competitor reviews, ratings, certifications, seller claims, popularity language, delivery promises or dietary/food claims.
- Record prices only as dated examples, not market truth or seed-data mandates.
- Treat absence as “not observed,” never proof of nonexistence.

## Limitations

- No user interviews, analytics, usability tests or purchasing were authorized; audience needs remain hypotheses.
- Search results, typo tolerance, filter mutation, cart persistence, checkout and focus movement were not exercised.
- Mobile checks covered one 390×844 viewport, not a full device/browser matrix.
- Accessible names were inspected from rendered DOM signals, but no assistive-technology session or formal WCAG audit was performed.
- Live sites change; all competitor observations expire as design evidence if materially relied upon after 90 days.
