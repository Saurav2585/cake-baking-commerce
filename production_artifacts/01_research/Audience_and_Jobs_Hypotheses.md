# Audience and Jobs Hypotheses

These are **hypotheses**, not validated personas or customer facts. Audience priority is an approved project decision; needs and behavior require later validation.

## H1 — Home baker / hobbyist (primary)

**Contexts:** learning a technique, baking for family or an occasion, replacing one ingredient, buying small quantities, choosing compatible colour/chocolate/tool formats.  
**Jobs:**

- Find the right product using familiar recipe or use-case language.
- Understand whether a product works with chocolate, cream, fondant, batter or another medium.
- Choose an affordable pack without excessive leftover.
- See ingredients, allergens, storage and handling when verified.
- Move from a recipe to a reviewable basket without needing professional terminology.

**Risks:** confusing compound with couverture; choosing water-based colour for fat-based use; unclear dimensions; buying an unsuitable pack; trusting unsupported claims.  
**Confidence:** Medium for task logic, Low for preference/priority.  
**Evidence:** E-006, E-013–E-017, E-020, E-027.

## H2 — Micro-bakery / home business (secondary)

**Contexts:** repeat production, batch costing, bulk/replenishment purchases, packaging to finished-product dimensions, delivery timing and heat-sensitive ingredients.  
**Jobs:**

- Compare price per standard unit and pack availability.
- Reorder consistent SKUs and scale recipe quantities.
- Match boxes/boards/pans to exact dimensions and counts.
- Assess stock, shelf life and handling without unsupported assurance.
- Review total basket implications before a simulated order.

**Risks:** inconsistent pack schemas, variant stock hidden at parent level, split or uncertain delivery, temperature damage, packaging dimensions interpreted incorrectly.  
**Confidence:** Medium for operational tasks, Low for demand frequency.  
**Evidence:** E-013, E-016–E-019, E-022, E-026–E-029.

## Shared jobs-to-be-done

| Situation | Job | Desired evidence in product experience |
|---|---|---|
| “I know the item” | Search by name, synonym, brand, pack or form | Grouped autosuggest and tolerant normalized tokens |
| “I know the bake, not the item” | Browse by task/use | Application facets and plain-language guidance |
| “I need the right variant” | Compare size, compatibility and price | Variant-specific stock/price/unit price plus category fields |
| “I am following a recipe” | Convert required amount to purchasable packs | Required/purchased/leftover quantities and substitution controls |
| “I need confidence” | Check handling and factual product details | Verified ingredients/allergens/storage/use, delivery simulation disclosure |
| “I use mobile/keyboard/zoom” | Complete the same task without loss | Reflow, semantic controls, focus, error/status feedback and adequate targets |

## Validation priorities

1. Comprehension of proposed department labels and colour/chocolate terminology.
2. Expected pack sizes for home versus micro-business use.
3. Importance of price per unit, stock, delivery and storage at decision time.
4. Recipe-to-cart expectation for optional ingredients, pantry items and substitutions.
5. Language/local terminology and search synonyms despite English-only UI.

## Prohibited promotion

Do not convert these hypotheses into testimonials, popularity statements, user counts, dietary claims or promises. Later teams may use them to frame flows, not as evidence that users behave this way.
