# Phase 5 Asset Pilot Brief

**Status:** Externally authorized as Phase 5A under D-028; execution completed for review
**Purpose:** Validate the asset and packaging system before bulk generation for the 24–30-product demo

## Pilot scope

Produce exactly the following coherent set, reusing masters where placements overlap:

1. **One homepage measured-ingredient composition** — 3:2 desktop master plus one 4:5 mobile art-directed crop; ingredient subjects must derive from approved pilot product/recipe data.
2. **One complete fictional product packaging family** — one parent dry ingredient with at least three weight variants (recommended 250 g, 500 g, and 1 kg only if the approved product record supports them); front/three-quarter packshot, consistent scale family study, label masters and exact-SKU exports.
3. **One representative department visual** — Ingredients department atlas tile, 3:2 and 1:1, visually related but not identical to the homepage composition.
4. **One recipe image** — approved representative recipe hero at 3:2 plus 4:3 listing crop; add one process crop only if it validates a real placement.
5. **One PDP media set** — for the pilot packaging family: primary, variant-owned pack views, ingredient macro, and pack-comparison study.
6. **One PLP thumbnail set** — 1:1 responsive outputs for all three pilot SKUs/representative parent state, including image-failure fallback verification.

This is the smallest useful pilot: it tests ingredient/editorial art direction, a reusable label system, variant truth, cross-route crop behavior, one department signature, accessibility decisions, and manifest lifecycle without starting bulk catalog production.

## Preconditions and inputs

- Phase 4B externally approved and Phase 5 explicitly authorized.
- One approved fictional product-brand direction and one factual parent product with three valid SKU records, including product form, exact net weights, pack geometry, and tri-state Ingredients/Allergens/Storage data.
- One approved recipe record and ingredient relationship; confirmed homepage/department placement sizes.
- Prototype/legal boundaries retained for Pantryform and fictional marks.
- Asset Manifest storage/validator location agreed; creators have no need to expose provider credentials.

## Generation boundary

Generation may be used only after the facts and briefs are locked. Prompts must avoid real brands, competitor packaging, certification/rating/trust symbols, unsupported food/health/dietary/origin/performance language, and unsafe handling. Generated label text is compositional reference only and must be rebuilt manually from approved copy. Do not extrapolate the full seven-department system during the pilot.

## Required evidence

- Contact sheet of candidates and rejection reasons; final masters and deterministic exports.
- Packaging front/side/back structural review, three-pack scale comparison, 160px/96px thumbnail captures, 320px PDP/PLP captures, 200% zoom, image-disabled and failure fallback.
- Desktop/mobile homepage, department and recipe placements.
- Grayscale/colour-vision and contrast checks for information surrounding imagery.
- Asset Manifest validation report; source/prompt/reference, licence, factual claims, trade-dress, alt decisions, dimensions/bytes/formats, optimization, SHA-256 checksums, approval and known limitations.

## Pilot acceptance gate

- The three SKUs are unmistakably one parent family yet accurately differentiated by text, motif and size—not colour alone.
- Pack weight, apparent scale, form, included contents and selected-SKU media are truthful.
- The set expresses measured ingredients → useful selection → joyful making and does not resemble a generic marketplace/Shopify pack wall.
- Ingredient Theatre adds sensory detail but product and recipe facts work with images absent.
- All crops survive required placements without misleading outpainting or clipped meaning.
- No unsupported claim, fake statutory/certification content, real brand/trade dress, unsafe depiction, or untracked asset remains.
- Every final file and derivative has an approved manifest record and passes technical/accessible QA.
- PM/human review explicitly approves, revises, or rejects bulk-production expansion. Pilot approval does not authorize automatic bulk generation.

## Stop conditions

Stop and return for correction if product data conflicts across artifacts; a generated asset changes ingredient form/count; a pack resembles a real competitor; licensing/provenance is incomplete; label copy would require invented legal facts; mobile crops misrepresent the subject; or accessible/product facts depend on the image.

## Out of scope

The remaining products/departments, full recipe library, real retailer identity production, commercial packaging compliance, animation assets, production application integration, paid stock acquisition, database upload, and bulk generation are excluded.
