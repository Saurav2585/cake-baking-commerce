# Phase 4B Readout

## Recommendation

Approve **Measured Transformation** as Pantryform’s motion direction: tactile but restrained motion makes ingredient arrival, measurement, pack selection and recipe transformation legible as one system. Editorial discovery may use longer staged sequences; utility and transactional pages use short, local feedback. Motion never owns facts, selection, navigation, errors or task completion.

## Demonstrated moments

- Homepage ingredients assemble around the fixed measurement study while the proposition enters as one bounded sequence.
- Raw → measured → made steps reveal in authored order without gating the section.
- Department-atlas shapes respond locally to hover/focus without moving labels or links.
- Ingredient Theatre uses a short staged reveal of premise, factual product card and decorative macro field.
- Recipe-to-supplies connectors express the four-stage transformation while rows remain complete.
- PDP pack choice restarts a bounded media/control response and keeps variant-owned facts atomic.
- Navigation/filter drawers use a contained short entrance with immediate Escape/focus recovery.
- Add feedback, error emphasis and confirmation each use a single local, cancelable response.

## Reduced/static evidence

The direct toggle and `motion=reduce`/`motion=off` modes remove every animation and transition while preserving final composition, focus, status and actions. The HTML contains an explicit no-JavaScript fallback; the underlying approved Phase 4A state is the visual baseline, so the motion stylesheet never owns content visibility until the enhancement script declares readiness.

Browser validation covered 14 screens at 1440, 1280, 1024, 768, 430, 390, 360 and 320 CSS px (112 route/width combinations), followed by 56 narrow-width regression checks after a review-control fix. All 14 screens passed deterministic 320px + 200% text + reduced-motion checks with zero animation/transition and no overflow. Rapid repeated Add/variant actions resolved to one latest state; drawer interruption returned focus to Close; portrait/landscape resize preserved the recipe review. Browser logs contained no warnings/errors.

## Performance evidence

The prototype uses only CSS transform/opacity/clip-path and small class/state updates, with no scroll hijack, autoplay media, external animation runtime or continuous loop. Motion is bounded by semantic durations, concurrency limits and the measurable production budget. A transformed drawer initially caused 8px temporary horizontal overflow; clipping was corrected at its backdrop boundary and revalidated immediately and at all narrow widths.

## Asset-system recommendation

Adopt the typed fail-closed Asset Manifest as the source for provenance, relationship, crop, alt, claim, optimisation, checksum/version and replacement history. Use manually designed retailer/packaging systems and CSS/SVG utility geometry; generate or license imagery only by approved family and record every source. Factual pack/media claims remain catalog-owned.

The packaging framework keeps Pantryform as retailer and fictional product brands as product metadata, with one scalable label grid across seven merchandise departments and explicit SKU/variant/net-quantity zones.

## Proposed Phase 5 pilot

Authorize only the bounded pilot after Phase 4B approval: one homepage ingredient composition, one complete three-variant fictional packaging family, one department visual, one recipe image, one PDP media set and its PLP thumbnails. Review truthfulness, crop resilience, visual coherence, manifest completeness and production cost before any bulk generation.

## Generic-motion test

**Pass.** Removing name, palette and product imagery still leaves measured assembly around a quantity axis, raw→measured→made sequencing, material/atlas response, pack-selection feedback and recipe need→quantity→pack→explicit-cart continuity. The motion would not transfer unchanged to a SaaS dashboard or fashion storefront.

## Unresolved risks

- Runtime performance budgets require production-device measurement; the static prototype is directional evidence.
- Exact imagery/generation/licensing choices wait for a separately authorized pilot.
- Motion can still regress into generic reveal patterns if engineering ignores signature choreography or concurrency rules.
- Font/image loading, real content dimensions, assistive-technology announcements and low-powered devices need implementation QA.
- Pantryform remains prototype-only pending formal commercial clearance.

## Approval question

Approve or revise the Phase 4B motion system, asset blueprint, packaging system, manifest contract, proposed Phase 5 pilot and isolated review evidence. Phase 5 production and application engineering remain blocked.
