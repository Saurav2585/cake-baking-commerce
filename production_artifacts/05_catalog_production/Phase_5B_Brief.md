# Phase 5B Brief — Catalog Production

Produce a bounded, India-focused demo catalog without changing approved taxonomy, commerce logic, UX or visual direction.

## Required output

- Exactly 24 parent products across the first seven departments: 6 / 4 / 4 / 3 / 3 / 2 / 2.
- Between 32 and 40 explicit sellable SKUs with INR demo prices and fixture availability.
- Six original demo recipes with deterministic ingredient-to-product mappings.
- Separate product identity, SKU commerce facts, authored content and recipe mappings.
- Fictional brands remain secondary to Pantryform and prototype-only.

## Truth boundary

All product identity, prices and availability are fictional demo data. Unknown food ingredients, allergens and storage use the approved `information_not_provided` state. No certification, dietary, health, popularity, review, sustainability, food-contact, performance or outcome claim may be inferred.

## Exclusions

No bulk asset generation, UI, code, live inventory, payment, legal clearance or Phase 6 work.
