# Fictional Product Brand System — Measureloom

**Status:** Prototype direction only—formal legal clearance pending.

## Identity and relationship

Measureloom names the way measured units form a useful material system. It is a fictional product brand, never the retailer or marketplace seller. Pantryform remains the primary retail context; the required lockup reads **“Measureloom / A Pantryform pilot brand.”** The mark combines a square measure frame with a rising folded-field line.

## Wordmark and typography

- Uppercase sans wordmark with generous tracking; system master uses Arial for deterministic portability.
- Product identity uses Georgia; operational facts use Arial.
- Never generate or raster-paint the wordmark; use the editable SVG master.

## Colour and scale

- Cocoa `#5B2F23`, ink `#241712`, cream `#F4EAD8`, paper `#FFFAF1`, saffron `#D8892D`, coral `#C75A44`.
- Monochrome cocoa-on-cream is the default. The mark never relies on colour to communicate SKU.
- Clear space: at least the cap height of the M around all sides. Minimum digital width: 120 px for the full lockup, 72 px for wordmark only. At smaller sizes, show the product name and weight instead.

## Prohibited use

Do not present Measureloom as seller, certification, endorsement or real manufacturer. Do not add quality, origin, cocoa, health, dietary or performance descriptors to the brand. Do not use scripts, crests, seals, metallic luxury effects, real-brand container silhouettes or certification-like roundels.
