## Visual candidates and rejection checks

`previews/asset_pf5a_selected_contact_sheet_v1.png` records the selected set. The initial generated foundations were accepted only as text-free editorial layers. Any generated implication of exact tool markings is not used as product fact. No generated packaging candidate was advanced; this avoids fake/distorted text and competitor trade dress entirely. Deterministic packaging versions with wrong pack weight, missing prototype disclosure, clipped medallion, colour-only differentiation or non-proportional family scale are build-blocking and were not retained.

The review explicitly checked generic appearance, ingredient form, pack scale, fake text, competitor resemblance, unsupported claims, crop meaning, thumbnail readability and Pantryform fit. Necessary rejection evidence is textual because discarded packaging was never generated or committed.
