# Creative Concepts

## 1. Measurefield — recommended

**Pantryform relevance:** physical measure rules turn cocoa into a working material, connecting raw ingredient, selection and method. **Packaging:** cream paper pouch, editorial label, large weight medallion and one/two/four measure bars. **Image:** warm top-down cocoa fields, metal tools and real particle edges. **Typography:** pragmatic sans metadata with a warm editorial serif product name. **Motif:** measured fields and proportional bars. **Department relation:** Ingredients owns cocoa/cream; the wider system can change matter and tool while retaining measurement logic. **Homepage/PDP/PLP:** expansive negative-space hero, factual pack studies and strong silhouette cards. **Mobile:** art-directed right focal crop; weight remains text outside and on pack. **Accessibility:** high contrast; variant differentiation uses text, size and bar count, not colour alone. **Trade-dress risk:** low after visual screening because no real pack was referenced; legal clearance remains required. **Strength:** unmistakably baking/measurement-specific. **Weakness:** paper-pouch structure is illustrative until real dimensions/material exist.

## 2. Ingredient Ledger

**Pantryform relevance:** treats product facts like an annotated pantry ledger. **Packaging:** strict ruled label with tabular variant block. **Image:** clinical ingredient specimens aligned to a baseline grid. **Typography:** mostly grotesk and monospaced numerals. **Motif:** rows, ticks and calibration notes. **Homepage/PDP/PLP:** high-information compositions and precise selectors. **Mobile:** collapses into stacked ledger panels. **Accessibility:** clear hierarchy and excellent textual differentiation. **Trade-dress risk:** low, but could resemble laboratory or specialty-coffee packaging. **Strength:** commerce clarity. **Weakness:** less warm and joyful; risks over-intellectualizing cocoa.

## 3. Folded Batch

**Pantryform relevance:** folded parchment becomes the transition from ingredient to make. **Packaging:** expressive diagonal fold with a bold quantity corner. **Image:** paper planes, powder seams and cropped tools. **Typography:** large serif phrases with compact labels. **Motif:** fold, reveal and batch boundary. **Homepage/PDP/PLP:** dramatic editorial crops and angled panels. **Mobile:** fold becomes a vertical edge. **Accessibility:** facts remain on flat high-contrast zones. **Trade-dress risk:** moderate because diagonal colour blocks are common in food packaging. **Strength:** memorable movement potential. **Weakness:** less scalable and more likely to compete with product facts.

## Selection

**Measurefield** is selected because it best carries Measured Joy, Working Pantry precision and Ingredient Theatre without borrowing a category cliché. The generic-template test passes: without Pantryform or colour, the measured powder fields, graduated pack family and recipe bridge remain recognizably about baking ingredients and making.
