# Three-SKU Packaging Family

## Family logic

The three cream paper-pouch illustrations share camera, light, material, wordmark, label grid and product naming. Their visible heights scale from 72% to 84% to 100% of the 1 kg master, creating an honest ordinal size relationship without asserting physical dimensions. Weight appears in both text and a dark medallion; one, two and four saffron bars provide a non-colour secondary cue.

## Boundaries

Paper material and pouch geometry are illustrative assumptions. Relative dimensions are not declared physical dimensions or content-volume relationships. No generative label text, statutory data, real-brand mark or unsupported claim is present.
