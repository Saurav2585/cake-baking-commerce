## Structure

The front has a 560×760 coordinate system with an 18-unit coral trim marker, 56-unit safe inset, subordinate product-brand zone, product identity, form, non-colour quantity coding, weight medallion, internal SKU and prototype disclosure. The side holds only locked identity plus explicit unknown critical facts. The structural back reserves an approved-information placeholder and lists excluded statutory content.

## Zones and rules

- **Quiet/trim:** 18-unit top trim reference; all live copy remains at least 44 units from the edge.
- **Brand:** Measureloom plus “A Pantryform pilot brand”; no seller implication.
- **Identity:** “Baking cocoa powder” and “Form · Powder.”
- **Variant:** 250 g / 500 g / 1 kg, internal SKU and one/two/four measure bars.
- **Critical facts:** Ingredients, Allergens and Storage each resolve to “Information not provided.”
- **Disclosure:** “Portfolio prototype · Not for sale.”
- **Excluded:** regulatory number, barcode, MRP, batch, dates, certifications, manufacturer and unapproved legal copy.

All copy is authored as SVG text from the Fact Lock. No AI-generated typography exists in final packaging. Production prepress, dielines, mandatory declarations and commercial compliance are out of scope.
