# Content and Microcopy Requirements

## Voice and evidence rules

Use clear Indian English: warm, capable and practical, with exact pack/quantity language. This is directional UX copy, not final campaign copy. Never invent certifications, reviews, testimonials, heritage, expertise, health/dietary assurances, quality superlatives, popularity, scarcity or live fulfillment facts. Fictional product brands remain subordinate to Pantryform, the prototype retailer.

## Content hierarchy

Lead with the user’s task, then the factual choice, then the next action. Put qualifications next to the claim/action they govern. Keep error copy specific: what happened, what was retained, and the recovery action. Use sentence case and familiar verbs.

## Required copy patterns

| Context | Directional pattern | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Demo price/availability | “Demo price” / “Demo availability: Available” | “Live price,” “Only 2 left,” urgency |
| Unknown critical fact | **“Information not provided”** under Ingredients, Allergens or Storage | blank section, “None,” inferred absence |
| Compatibility | “Suitable for [explicit supported use]. Check the product details before choosing.” | inferred suitability or guaranteed results |
| Variant | “[250 g pack] — ₹[x] demo price”; unit price where meaningful | price detached from pack basis |
| Recipe quantity | “Required: 600 g · Selected: 500 g pack × 2 · Purchased: 1 kg · Leftover: 400 g” | hiding surplus or presenting lowest unit cost as default |
| Pantry-owned | “I already have this” with reversible state | prechecked omission without explanation |
| Optional/tool | “Optional—not selected” / “Tool suggestion—not selected” | automatic add |
| Substitution | “Using [substitute] instead of [original]. [approved factual note].” | silent replacement or unverified equivalence |
| Unavailable/unmapped | “No available mapped product. Choose an option or mark this as already owned.” | disappearing ingredient |
| Add/merge | “Added [quantity] × [product, variant]. Cart now has [result].” | generic “Success!” alone |
| Price change | “Demo price changed from ₹[old] to ₹[new]. Review before continuing.” | silent repricing |
| Search zero | “No results for ‘[query]’.” Offer clear filters, spelling, departments and Recipes | blame, invented recommendations/popularity |
| Recoverable error | “[Action] wasn’t completed. Your selections are still here. Try again.” | “Something went wrong” alone |
| Persistence limitation | “Saved in this browser for this demo.” | account/cloud-sync implication |
| Checkout disclosure | **“This is a portfolio demo. No payment or real order will be created.”** | disclosure only in footer |
| Totals | “Demo item total”; Shipping/Tax: **“Not calculated in this demo”** | “Amount due,” ₹0 shipping/tax |
| Final action | **“Complete demo checkout”** | Pay, Buy now, Place order |
| Confirmation | **“Demo checkout complete”** then **“No payment was taken and no real order was placed.”** | Receipt, invoice, unqualified order confirmed |

## Page and component content requirements

- Product cards: product name, subordinate fictional brand, selected/default pack where unambiguous, demo price, meaningful unit price, demo availability, wishlist state and Add/Select options.
- PDP purchase area: identity, selected variant, pack quantity/dimensions, demo and unit price, availability, quantity, action, and critical handling/compatibility facts before supporting prose.
- Product facts: Ingredients, Allergens and Storage headings remain visible with exact unknown fallback; family attributes use catalog terms and units.
- PLP/search: query/category, result count, applied filters, sort, recovery. Do not label rails “best sellers” without approved evidence.
- Recipe review: selected servings, every ingredient state, required/selected/purchased/leftover, demo price, override/reset and omissions summary. Explain that pack matching is smallest sufficient, not cheapest per unit.
- Cart: variant/SKU label, unit price, pack quantity, source recipe where applicable, changes/issues, subtotal and demo boundaries.

## Labels, instructions and errors

- Button labels name the outcome and, where useful, count: “Add 6 selected ingredients,” “Apply filters (18 results),” “Remove cocoa powder.” Icon-only controls still need an accessible name.
- Hints state format before entry; errors do not rely on examples alone. Do not use placeholder text as the only label.
- Empty states preserve orientation and provide one or two relevant recovery paths; error states distinguish unavailable data from no content.
- Destructive-looking local actions name their target and provide reversible recovery where feasible.
- Announcements are concise and omit decorative language so repeated use remains understandable.

## Content review checklist

Check every claim against catalog/approved strategy; verify INR/Indian number formatting and unit consistency; confirm demo qualifier proximity; search for prohibited real-commerce verbs; ensure no blank unknown critical fields; test long product/variant names at 320px; test errors and live announcements out of visual context.
