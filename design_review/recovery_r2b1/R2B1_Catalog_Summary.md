## Replacement / resolution log

R1 proposed 48 products, one of which — "Round Silver Cake Boards (18-Inch, Pack of 6)" — had no confirmable manufacturer/brand attribution ("Unclear / not confirmed — generic retail listing without a stated manufacturer brand"). Per R2B1 Section 2, that entry is **replaced** with **PME Cake Drum Board** (department unchanged: Cake Boards, Boxes & Packaging). PME's brand identity is independently verified elsewhere in the same register (PME Supatube Piping Tip, PME Edible Sugar Pearls), and PME genuinely sells cake drum boards as part of its Sugarcraft range. No other R1 product was replaced — all 47 remaining R1 identities carried through unchanged.

Two pre-existing facts were corrected during this migration (both carried forward from earlier phases, re-confirmed here):

- **JVG "Chocolate Vermicelli Sprinkles" → "Rainbow Sprinkles"** (R2A correction, retained): the R1-staged image depicts loose rainbow sprinkles, not the brown vermicelli variant originally registered. The listing was retitled to the already-verified "JVG Rainbow Sprinkles" R1 entry (same brand, price, pack size) rather than swapping in an unverified image.
- **Callebaut 811 pack size**: the staged packshot's own label reads "NET WEIGHT ... 1 kg – 2.2 lbs", not the 400 g this line was priced at in R1/R2A. The canonical record now lists 1 kg (₹2,200 demo fixture) as the primary/default variant, with 400 g (₹950, the R1-registered price) kept as a genuine second pack-size variant using the product-level image (no fabricated variant-specific photo).
