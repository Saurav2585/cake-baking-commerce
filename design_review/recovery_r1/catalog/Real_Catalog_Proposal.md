# Real Catalog Proposal — Pantryform

This document proposes 48 real, currently-sold baking-supply products from the Indian market, sourced and verified through manufacturer sites, brand storefronts, and major Indian retailers/marketplaces, for use as provenance-backed reference stock in the fictional demo ecommerce catalog "Pantryform." Products are grouped into 7 departments and span 30 distinct real brands (Callebaut, Morde, Van Houten, Amul, Weikfield, Hershey's, Pillsbury, Urban Platter, Eagle, CCDS, Magic Colours, Colourmist, Puramate, Wilton, Bakersville, Tropolite, Rich's, FunCakes, Nutella/Ferrero, JVG, PME, Borosil, Wonderchef, Ateco, Lukzer, Cake Craft Shop, The Baker's Mart, Eco Bags India, Famous Packaging, and Pirsq), plus one item whose retail brand attribution could not be confirmed. Full per-product sourcing, descriptions, and provenance notes live in the companion file `Product_Provenance_Register.md`; this file is the readable summary. All prices are demo fixtures, not scraped live pricing.
