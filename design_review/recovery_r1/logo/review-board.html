Pantryform logo review board

Recovery R1 — three genuinely distinct identity directions for Pantryform, each tested as symbol, lockup, wordmark, dark/light/monochrome treatment, favicon scale, minimum-size legibility, and in-context header, mobile-header and product-card placement. No direction is a plain typed word, generic cupcake/chef-hat clip-art, or a script bakery font. The production header logo has not been changed — this page is an isolated review artifact only.

Concept A — Measure Mark

Geometric letterform

A Pantryform "P" whose bowl is drawn as a measuring scoop, with three graduation ticks standing in for the counter of the letter. Reads as initial + tool in one stroke; bold enough to survive at favicon size, with the tick detail rewarding closer inspection at editorial sizes. Wordmark pairs it with a confident, tight, all-caps-adjacent grotesque.

Concept B — Pantry Arch

Architectural container mark

A rounded cabinet-arch silhouette cut into three shelves, with a small jar resting on the lowest one — the pantry itself as the mark, not a letterform. Warmer and more domestic than Concept A; pairs with an italic serif wordmark that echoes the site's editorial display type.

Concept C — Maker's Label

Transformation / packaging mark

A hang-tag label silhouette with a punched hole, crossed by a diagonal line running from a raw-ingredient dot to a measured tick — literally "raw material becomes finished bake" as a pictogram. The most commerce/packaging-forward of the three; pairs with a tight, uppercase, wide-tracked wordmark suited to a label context.
